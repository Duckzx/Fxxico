## 2026-03-24 - Animated Side Drawer Accessibility & Focus Management
**Learning:** Animated sidebar drawers (CSS `transform: translateX`) require a small delayed buffer (e.g. 150ms timeout) before shifting focus to inner elements (like the close button). Browsers may fail to focus elements or handle screen-reader visibility if `.focus()` is called instantly before the transition starts. In addition, conditional 'Escape' key logic must inspect the drawer state (e.g., presence of '.show' class) to prevent focus jumping when the drawer is already closed.
**Action:** When working with CSS transition-based drawers/modals in vanilla JS, always wrap the initial `.focus()` call in a 100ms-200ms `setTimeout` aligned with the transition timing, and always verify state attributes before executing keyboard event actions.
